## What Changed Today

### 1) Changxin Technology moved into the scheduled IPO-offering stage — High priority

On **2026-07-09**, Changxin Technology Group (长鑫科技, proposed STAR Market code **688825**) disclosed its IPO prospectus, prospectus appendix, prospectus notice, and issuance-arrangement / preliminary-inquiry announcement. The disclosed timetable sets both online and offline subscription for **2026-07-16**; the online subscription code is **787825**. The prospectus states an initial public offering of **6,688,088,608 shares** (10.00% of post-offering capital before greenshoe), with a 15% over-allotment option. The issuer's proposed fundraising plan remains RMB **29.5bn** for memory-wafer-line technical upgrade, DRAM technology upgrade, and forward-looking DRAM R&D projects.

This is a material, confirmed change from the prior checkpoint's “registration effective / awaiting issuance documents” status. It makes the next week’s inquiry, pricing, subscription, allotment and listing documents the immediate catalysts.
